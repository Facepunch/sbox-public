Build, create and explore in our official Sandbox game.

It's like Garry's Mod — we provide you with the tools and you can do whatever you want with no predefined goals or objectives.

- Spawn props and entities from the cloud to build whatever you want.
- Use your Toolgun to weld pieces together, add wheels, thrusters, balloons, hydraulics and more.
- Explore community-made contraptions or make your own and share them.
- Mounts let you spawn content from supported games.

Controls

- Q — Opens the Spawn Menu, for spawning entities, picking tools, and controlling everything.
- C — Opens the Context Menu. Look at objects in the world to edit them.
- X — Toggle First / Third Person.
- TAB — Scoreboard.
- ENTER — Chat.
- V — Voice Chat.
- SPACE — Jump / Noclip if pressed twice.
